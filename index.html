FAQ

Will my website go down while you work on it?

I’ll review your setup and plan the changes to minimize disruption. Where possible, I’ll test on a separate copy first. If downtime is needed, we’ll agree on it before work begins.

Do you need my passwords?

Please don’t send passwords in the form or by email. If access is needed, I’ll explain what and why. Where your platform supports it, you can invite me with a separate account and remove access when the work is done.

What if you can’t fix it?

I’ll review the issue before proposing work and flag anything that needs more investigation. If a paid diagnostic is needed, we’ll agree on its scope and price first. If the work uncovers a limitation, we’ll discuss the options before proceeding.

How fast is the turnaround?

It depends on the scope, access and current availability. I’ll confirm a timeline with your quote. If you have a deadline, include it in your request.

How much will it cost?

Small fixes start at $199. Complete websites and larger updates are quoted individually. You’ll approve the scope and price before work begins, including any changes we agree on along the way.

Privacy notice

Your details are emailed to Service Boost through Google Workspace to respond to your request. Please don’t include passwords or sensitive information.

If you arrive through an outreach link, we record its reference, visit time, and accepted quote requests to understand which outreach is useful. The reference is privately associated with the prospect and campaign. We use temporary browser storage to avoid counting repeat visits in the same tab. Attribution records expire 90 days after the link is created and are removed within the next day. We do not record what you type for analytics. You can remove the ref parameter from the URL before using the site to avoid this tracking.
