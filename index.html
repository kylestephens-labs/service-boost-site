FAQ

When do I pay?

Half to get started ($750), and half when your redesign launches. You approve the design before anything goes live — the second half isn't due until you're happy with it.

How long does it take?

Most redesigns launch within 2–3 weeks. The main variable is how quickly you review the design — fast feedback means a faster launch.

Will my website stay online while you work?

I’ll review your setup and plan the changes to minimize disruption. Where possible, I’ll test on a separate copy first. If downtime is needed, I’ll agree on it with you before work begins.

Can you work with my current website platform?

Send me your website and I’ll review the platform before confirming the scope. I’ll flag any limitations or access requirements before work begins. Please don’t send passwords in the form or by email.

What if I need more than four pages?

The package covers up to four pages. If you need more, I’ll discuss the additional scope and price with you before any work begins.

Are hosting and ongoing support included?

The package covers the redesign and launch. I’ll confirm any hosting, third-party service costs or ongoing support separately before work begins.
