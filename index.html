What this service covers

Help with dropouts, weak coverage, router or mesh setup, and devices that won’t connect.

  • Identify whether the issue is the connection, network or device.
  • Configure compatible equipment and test where you use it.
  • Leave a summary of changes and next steps.

ISP faults, replacement hardware and fixed wiring may need a separate provider. A fix isn’t guaranteed.

Setup and configuration for computers, monitors, docks, webcams and connected office equipment.

  • Connect compatible devices and configure the essentials.
  • Test the work tasks you need to do.
  • Explain the setup and leave useful next steps.

Employer-managed equipment requires your IT team’s authorization. Hardware repair, fixed wiring and ongoing monitoring are not included.

Help with slow performance, software problems, updates, email and syncing.

  • Investigate what is interrupting your work.
  • Review settings and make agreed software changes.
  • Test the original task with you.

No internal hardware repair or data recovery. Failing equipment may need a repair specialist; performance improvements can’t be guaranteed.

Help connecting compatible printers and scanners to your computer, phone or network.

  • Review connections, drivers and print settings.
  • Configure supported printing or scanning features.
  • Test from your actual device.

Connection and software support only. Mechanical faults, parts and printer hardware repair need a repair provider.

Help with new-device setup, apps, email, syncing and everyday settings.

  • Connect your device and configure supported apps.
  • Review accessibility and settings with you.
  • Guide supported transfers when accounts and backups are available.

You enter your credentials. No screen or battery repair, account-lock bypass or recovery of lost data.

Setup and configuration for compatible smart speakers, lights, plugs, hubs and streaming devices.

  • Connect supported devices to your network and apps.
  • Configure the features you want to use.
  • Walk through everyday controls together.

No fixed wiring, electrical work, drilling or mounting. Physical installation and device compatibility are assessed before accepting work.

You don’t need to diagnose it first. Describe what you’re trying to do, what happens instead, and the devices involved.
