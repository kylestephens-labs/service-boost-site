Frequently asked questions

How much does support cost?

Choose a service to see its fixed price, included work and exclusions before requesting a visit. If you need work outside the selected package, we’ll agree on the scope and price before proceeding.

When and how do I pay?

Payment is due after your visit. No card is required to book. I’ll confirm available payment options before your appointment.

Which cities do you serve?

I serve Walnut Creek, Pleasant Hill, Concord, Lafayette, Orinda, Moraga, Alamo, Danville, Martinez, Clayton and San Ramon. Outside these cities? Ask Kyle before booking.

How do I schedule a visit?

Choose a service, select an available time, and enter your contact details and visit address in Calendly. Complete the booking to receive your confirmation. If you’re unsure which service fits, ask Kyle.

What if you can’t resolve the issue?

I’ll explain what I found and recommend next steps. The $229 troubleshooting visit covers diagnosis and work on one computer or printer issue, even if it can’t be fully resolved. Internal hardware repairs, data recovery, electrical work and fixed wiring aren’t included. We’ll agree on any additional work and its price before proceeding.

More FAQs
What can you help me with?

I help set up computers, set up and troubleshoot printers, configure routers, set up phones and tablets, and connect compatible smart-home devices. Choose a category to see the available services and what’s included. Not sure which one fits? Ask Kyle.

Do you help homes and small businesses?

Yes. I help homes, home offices and small businesses. If your employer manages the equipment, you’ll need your IT team’s authorization before I make changes.

Do you come to my home?

Yes. I provide on-site help for homes and home offices in the communities listed under “Which cities do you serve?”. Enter your full visit address when you book.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Please contact me as soon as possible if your plans change. There’s currently no cancellation fee.

Do I need to buy new equipment first?

You don’t need to buy anything before asking for help. For a setup visit, the device and any required equipment should be ready. If you’re unsure what to buy, share the make and model or ask me before purchasing so we can discuss compatibility.

Will you need my passwords or access to my files?

Please don’t send passwords in the request form, by email or by text. You enter your own credentials. We’ll discuss any access needed for the agreed work, and you should back up important files before changes are made.
