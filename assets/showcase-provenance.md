# Redesign portfolio showcase imagery

The seven `*-showcase.webp` images were generated with the built-in image tool
on 2026-10-07 using the corresponding existing `*-preview.jpg` as screen
references. Laptop and phone compositions follow the approved homepage mockup.
Each original generated PNG was converted to WebP, quality 88, maximum 1440 × 900.
These are illustrative concept presentations, not literal mobile screenshots,
client work or evidence of business results. Full concept links remain authoritative.

Sources: landscape-preview.jpg, salon-preview.jpg, auto-preview.jpg,
restaurant-preview.jpg, dental-preview.jpg, contractor-preview.jpg,
real-estate-preview.jpg. The founder photograph and brand logo remain the
existing supplied assets.

`homepage-share-v2.jpg` is a generated social preview graphic using the approved
headline and price, exported at 1200 × 630.

The additional check, chevron-left, chevron-right, arrow-right, shield-check,
plus and minus icons are from Tabler Icons (outline, fetched 2026-10-07).
Their MIT license is retained in icons/LICENSE.
